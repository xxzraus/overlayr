# Roadmap

Ideas that fit Overlayr's goal: make the desktop feel personal and get common tasks out of the way.

## Next
- **Real playback queue** for players that expose one, or through a local player API.
- **Multi-monitor support**: a theme and an overlay per display, brightness for every monitor.
- **Colour temperature** through an ICC profile instead of the amber filter.
- **Profiles export/import** together with the dock layout.
- **Animated wallpaper transition** when switching themes.

## Later
- **Clipboard history** with search and pinned items.
- **Window manager**: half/third snapping and moving windows between monitors with a hotkey.
- **Screenshots and screen recording** from the launcher.
- **File search** in the launcher.
- **Quick notes** and timers in the overlay.
- **Optional Spotify/SoundCloud keys** in settings for users who have their own API credentials.
- **Code signing** for the installer.

## Not planned
- UWP display-name localisation (unreliable and language-dependent).
- Bundled third-party wallpapers or music.
